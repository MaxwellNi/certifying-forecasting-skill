# Rights and attribution

The MIT license covers the authors' software and repository instructions.
It does not grant a separate license to `paper.pdf` or `technical-details.pdf`.
Rights in the research manuscripts remain with their respective rights holders.

Public data and third-party components retain their stated licenses and
attribution requirements. See [data access and licenses](DATA_ACCESS.md) and
the notices supplied with each study and dependency. Restricted source
observations and individual predictions are not distributed.

The repository's publication snapshot and checksums identify the shared files;
they do not constitute an IEEE copyright-transfer or PDF eXpress receipt.
